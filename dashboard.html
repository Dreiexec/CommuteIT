ⓘAbout CommuteIT
ABOUT COMMUTEIT

Built for smarter, greener trips.

CommuteIT brings route planning, live navigation, travel comparisons, and sustainability insights together for Metro Manila commuters.

•

Live navigation

Track your journey with GPS and follow your recommended route.

•

Multi-modal planning

Compare MRT, LRT, bus, jeepney, and other travel choices.

•

Eco-Score

See estimated CO₂ savings and sustainability performance per trip.

•

Travel reports

Review your trip history and environmental impact over time.

•

Create your account

Set up your commuter profile and keep your preferences available for your own experience.

•

Choose a route

Compare travel time, fare, and Eco-Score before you travel.

•

Start navigation

Use the interactive map and live GPS tracking while commuting.

•

Review your impact

Your completed trips update your dashboard and sustainability information.
